## 1. Risk Classification Rationale

Although `L02A`–`L03A` deliver offline pure-JVM HOSLAM codec and synthetic replay UI (`T2`/`T3`), the overall `LOC-001` initiative reaches **`T4 (Critical Risk)`** because `L04A`–`L05` introduce:
1. A dedicated `local` Android build variant (`com.weback.robot.local`) with `android.permission.INTERNET` and full composition-root source-set separation (`src/cloudPilot/` vs `src/local/`).
2. Protected modifications to `policies/checks.json`, `policies/checks.baseline.json`, and `verification/checks/apk_validator.py` (plus post-merge CI policy SHA synchronization).
3. Future human-supervised hardware-in-the-loop observation (`HIL-01`..`HIL-06`) against a physical Rapido R8S vacuum.

In Stage `L01` / `L01F` / `L01F2`, **zero** `T4` policy, code, Gradle, CI, or manifest changes are applied.
