## 1. Mandatory Pre-Live Offline Physical-Device Checklist (`TEST-PILOT-469` — `NOT_RUN`)

### Preconditions & Evidence Hygiene
1. Verify SHA-256 checksums of `weback-fct004c-offline-debug.apk`, `weback-fct004c-pilot.apk`, and `weback-fct004c-pilot-androidTest.apk` against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before transfer.
2. Explicit human authorization is required before any physical Android >= 12 (`minSdk = 31`) device execution.
3. Zero production DNS queries, zero real credentials, zero real account identifiers, and zero live cloud connections during all `TEST-PILOT-469` steps.
4. **Screenshot & Public Evidence Protection:** Never capture or export screenshots containing actual account identifiers, target `thing_name`, or passwords into public evidence. During `FLAG_SECURE`-protected screens (`Review Live Pilot Authorization` and credential entry), `screencap` must be rejected/blacked out by the OS.

## 2. Minimal First-Live-Attempt Operating Procedure (`TEST-PILOT-414` — `NOT_RUN`)

**Do NOT execute until `TEST-PILOT-469` Steps 1–12 ALL pass AND a separate external countersigned [external-pilot-authorization.md](external-pilot-authorization.md) is approved (`product_owner_approved: true`, `lead_maintainer_approved: true`, `offline_device_checklist_passed: true`, `physical_device_monotonic_expiry_verified: true`).**

1. **Out-of-Band Provisioning:** Verify `com.weback.robot.pilot` APK SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and the external approval record (`verified_pilot_apk_sha256`). For the initial authentication-only observation, set `pinned_api_origin: null`, `pinned_wss_origin: null`, and `endpoint_provenance_ref: null`. Provision `authorized_pilot_record.json` and `authorized_pilot_record.sha256` (containing NO password) into app-private `no_backup/pilot_external_auth/` via `adb shell run-as com.weback.robot.pilot`.
2. **Launch & Review:** Launch `com.weback.robot.pilot` (confirm `OFFLINE_DEFAULT`), open **Pilot Readiness (Offline / Live Review)** -> **Review Live Pilot Authorization**, confirm `FLAG_SECURE` active, inspect the read-only provisioned scope, enter the out-of-band 64-hex `record_sha256` from the external approval ticket, and tap **Verify & Activate**.
3. **Human Credential Entry:** Verify **Real-Server Authentication** UI and `FLAG_SECURE` active; human operator enters authorized calling code, dedicated isolated test account identifier, and password directly; tap **Authenticate Live Account (1 Attempt)** (dispatches **at most 1** `POST /prod/oauth` with `opt="login"`, `api_version="6.1.7"`, `app_name="WeBack"`, `client_id="yugong_app"`, `language="en"`, UTF-8 over TLS, `<= 15s` timeout, `0` retries).
4. **Outcome Branching:**
   - **If Unapproved Dynamic Origins Returned:** App retains ONLY sanitized `ObservedOriginSummary` (`apiOrigin`, `wssOrigin`), discards untrusted session/token, revokes authorization, initiates cleanup, and returns to `OFFLINE_DEFAULT` (`UNTRUSTED_ORIGIN_REJECTED`) with **zero** subsequent network requests. Record the sanitized endpoint observation for offline provenance review; **stop** (do not retry).
   - **If Challenge/Error Returned:** App aborts fail-closed (`CHALLENGE_REQUIRED` / `PROTOCOL_MISMATCH_ABORT` / `AUTH_REJECTED`), revokes authorization, and returns to `OFFLINE_DEFAULT`; **stop** (do not retry).
   - **If Independently Pre-Approved Pinned Origins Returned:** App displays sanitized authentication result (`AUTHENTICATED_INVENTORY_READY`). Tap **Fetch Target Inventory (Single Read-Only Request)** (dispatches **at most 1** `POST` `user_thing_list_get`, **0** WebSocket connections). Inspect sanitized target robot capability summary (`INVENTORY_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY`).
5. **Mandatory Teardown:** Tap **Emergency Disconnect & Wipe** immediately after recording sanitized observation; confirm return to `OFFLINE_DEFAULT`, `storageCleared=true`, and zero leaked credentials.
